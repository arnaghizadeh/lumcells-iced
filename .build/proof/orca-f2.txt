What Orca said for the examples, lane F2
========================================

Orca 46.1 on this workstation (Linux Mint 22.3), 23 Nov 2026, against target/debug/focus_ring,
screen_reader and accessible_names (iced_winit with the `accessibility` feature, AccessKit over
AT-SPI), made with `.github/scripts/orca-read.sh <program> <keys> <out>`.

Each run was in a session of its own (Xvfb display with openbox, its own D-Bus session bus and
AT-SPI bus, its own home and runtime directory, speech sent to an ALSA device that plays
nothing). Nothing was spoken on the speakers and NOBODY LISTENED: the lines marked "said" are
Orca's own record of what it sent to the speech synthesizer ("SPEECH OUTPUT" in its debug log).
The lines in brackets are the keys xdotool sent, one every 1.8 s. Orca's echo of the key itself
("tab", "return", "space", "left shift", "escape") is left out.

What it shows that is new since F1's orca-focus_ring.txt:
- a slider is "Volume slider 40." and each arrow says the new number;
- a scrollable that is a stop says "panel." and reads what is inside it;
- a combo box is "Language combo box.", its open list is "list box" with the option under the keys;
- a pick list that is opened says "list box" and the option; a named one is called by its name;
- a text editor is "Notes entry."; the arrows read the character and the line under the caret,
  Shift+Left says the character and "selected", Home "Text unselected.";
- a status line (`text(..).live(Live::Polite)`) is said when its words change while the focus is
  elsewhere: "Syncing 3 files", "Syncing 2 files";
- the dialog of focus_ring: Tab says "Keep it", "Clear it", "Keep it" (the trap), nothing of the form;
- the names of F1/S5: "Open Work push button.", "Sync Work check box partially checked.",
  "Password password text." (nothing of what was typed in it), "Start at login toggle button".

Not said, or odd:
- the very first Tab from the window to the first widget (as in F1); in Orca's log it finds the
  focus already moved when it handles the key and takes it without announcing. Not solved.
- the option chosen with Return in an open list, and the new value of the closed list after it;
- Page Down and End in a focused scrollable: nothing about how far it is scrolled;
- "Hide the notice" was said when the button under the focus changed its name, as a live text is;
- a field is called by its placeholder as its content when it is empty ("Computer name entry Mac.");
- once, in an earlier run under load, Orca was 19 s late and said the first stops out of order;
  that run is not kept.
